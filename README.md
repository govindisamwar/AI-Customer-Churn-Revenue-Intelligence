\# AI-Powered Customer Churn \& Revenue Intelligence Platform



\## Project Overview



An end-to-end Data Science and AI platform designed to predict customer churn, identify churn drivers, analyze customer value, and estimate revenue at risk.



\## Objectives



\- Predict customers likely to churn

\- Identify important churn factors

\- Segment customers based on risk and value

\- Calculate revenue at risk

\- Provide actionable customer retention insights

\- Build interactive business dashboards

\- Deploy the prediction system through Streamlit



\## Technology Stack



\- Python

\- Pandas

\- NumPy

\- Scikit-learn

\- XGBoost

\- SHAP

\- SQL

\- Power BI

\- Streamlit

\- Plotly

\- Jupyter Notebook



\## Project Modules



\### Data Engineering

Data collection, cleaning, preprocessing, and database preparation.



\### Data Analytics

Exploratory data analysis, visualization, customer behavior analysis, and feature engineering.



\### AI \& Machine Learning

Customer churn prediction, model training, evaluation, and explainability.



\### Revenue Intelligence

Revenue-at-risk analysis, customer segmentation, Power BI dashboard, and Streamlit application.
